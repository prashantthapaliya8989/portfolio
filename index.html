About

👋 Welcome to my portfolio

I'm Prashant Thapaliya, a Product Engineer at Esri on the Desktop Architecture team, where I help build configuration frameworks and UI for tools that let GIS professionals tailor their own ArcGIS applications. I work on testing and QA to keep that framework reliable as it evolves. I came to this role from public safety GIS, having previously worked as a GIS Analyst at LETA911 supporting real-time emergency communication systems in northern Colorado. That mission-driven grounding now shapes how I think about building flexible, powerful, and accessible geospatial tools for the people who use them.

I hold an MS degree in Geography with a concentration in Data Analytics from the University of North Texas, and a Bachelors in Geomatics Engineering from Kathmandu University. I am proficient in tools like ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Experience Builder, ArcGIS Survey123, Tableau, and Python. The journey as a Geomatics Engineer has developed my passion to address critical environmental management, public safety and emergency response.

Behind this profile, there is a genuine person. In my leisure time, you might find me: hiking, travelling, reading about technology, playing guitar and ukulele, involving in social organizations or playing football.
